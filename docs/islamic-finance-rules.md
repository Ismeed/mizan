# Islamic Finance Rule Engine

## Mirath (Inheritance) Rules
Based on the Quran (Surah An-Nisa 4:11-12, 4:176).

### Fard Shares
- Husband: 1/2 (no children), 1/4 (with children)
- Wife: 1/4 (no children), 1/8 (with children)
- Daughter: 1/2 (if single), 2/3 (if multiple)
- Son: Residuary (Asabah)

### Blocking (Hajb)
- Son blocks grandson, brothers, and sisters.
- Father blocks grandfather and brothers.

## Zakat Rules
- Nisab for Gold: 85 grams
- Nisab for Silver: 595 grams
- Rate: 2.5% for lunar year (Hawl).
