# AI Safety & Governance Restrictions (Phase 15)

## Non-Negotiable AI Restrictions

1. **mustNotRecalculate**: The AI Assistant must not recalculate Mirath or Zakat.
2. **mustNotChangeDecision**: The AI Assistant must not change Hijab decisions, fixed fractions, residuary decisions, rates, nisab results, or obligations.
3. **mustNotChangeMadhhab**: The AI Assistant must not silently switch madhhab.
4. **mustNotInventEvidence**: The AI Assistant must not invent evidence records.
5. **mustNotInventSourceText**: The AI Assistant must not invent Qur’an, Hadith, or scholarly text.
6. **mustNotInventTranslation**: The AI Assistant must not invent unapproved translations.
7. **mustNotInventRule**: The AI Assistant must not invent rule conditions or decisions.
8. **mustNotInventException**: The AI Assistant must not invent exceptions.
9. **mustNotPresentCommentaryAsEvidence**: Generated commentary must never be presented as direct source text or Qur’an/Hadith quotation.
10. **mustNotUseUnapprovedComparativeContext**: Comparative madhhab explanations are allowed only when an approved Comparative Record exists.
11. **mustUseProvidedVerifiedContext**: The AI must rely solely on the server-hydrated context package.
12. **mustDiscloseInsufficientContext**: The AI must disclose when available context is insufficient to answer a question.
