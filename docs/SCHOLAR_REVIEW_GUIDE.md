# MIZAN Scholar Review & Sharia Approval Guide

## Overview for Islamic Scholars (Muftis & Fuqaha)

As a Sharia Reviewer for MIZAN, your role is to verify the jurisprudential accuracy and Quranic/Hadith evidence for all structured knowledge items.

## Verification Checklist

1. **Madhhab Attribution**: Confirm that the ruling is accurately attributed to the specified school (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali, or Ja'fari).
2. **Evidence Resolution**: Verify that the linked Quranic ayah or Hadith citation directly supports the stated ruling.
3. **Conditions & Exceptions**: Check that all prerequisites (e.g., Hawl, Nisab, presence of blocking heirs) are explicitly captured.
4. **Calculability**: Ensure the rule can be evaluated deterministically without ambiguity.
5. **No AI Inference**: Confirm that calculations rely on fixed mathematical formulas, not generative model output.
