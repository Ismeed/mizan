# Hadith Evidence Standard (Phase 4)

## Specification

Hadith evidence records are identified by permanent IDs in the format `HADITH-<COLLECTION>-<CANONICAL_NUMBER>` (e.g. `HADITH-BUKHARI-001454`).

## Key Components

1. **Canonical Numbering**: Authoritative internal baseline number.
2. **Edition-Specific Numbering**: Array of edition variations (volume, book, chapter, page, publisher).
3. **Attributed Scholar Grading**: Dedicated grading records linked to specific scholars and sources.
4. **Isnad & Matn**: Separation of chain of narration (`isnad`) and main text (`matn`).
