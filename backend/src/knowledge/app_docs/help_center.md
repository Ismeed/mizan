# Help Center & Knowledge Base

## Purpose
The primary purpose of Help Center & Knowledge Base is to provide users with seamless access to MIZAN features, workflows, and calculations.

## Who Uses It
- All registered users, guests, scholars, and administrators seeking assistance with help center & knowledge base.

## How to Access It
- Can be accessed by navigating to route `/help` or tapping the designated card/tab in MIZAN.

## Navigation Path
- `Dashboard -> Help Center & Knowledge Base (/help)`

## Buttons & Icons
- **Primary Button**: Action button to proceed with workflow.
- **Header Back Icon**: Return to previous screen.
- **Settings Gear**: Access preferences.

## Available Actions
- Perform calculations, view breakdowns, export PDFs, edit options, or consult the AI Assistant.

## Input Fields & Validation Rules
- Required fields must be completed. Numerical inputs must be non-negative.

## Possible Errors & Success Messages
- **Success**: "Action completed successfully."
- **Error**: "Invalid input or network connection error."

## Related Screens
- Dashboard, Settings, AI Assistant, History, Profile.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Q: Where do I find this screen?** Navigate via `/help`.

## Tips & Troubleshooting
- Ensure your device is connected to internet for AI sync, or rely on offline Rule Engine calculations.

## Developer Notes
- Maintained under `apps/mobile/` or `backend/src/`.
